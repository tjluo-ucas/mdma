## Core spreadsheet and management-science texts

1. **Winston & Albright, _Practical Management Science_, 6e.** Closely aligned with the course's Excel, optimization, simulation, and management-case spine. [Cengage](https://www.cengage.com/c/practical-management-science-6e-winston-albright/9781337406659/)
2. **Ragsdale, _Spreadsheet Modeling and Decision Analysis_, 9e.** Modeling, optimization, decision analysis, simulation, and business analytics. [Cengage](https://faculty.cengage.com/titles/9780357132098)
3. **Powell & Baker, _Business Analytics: The Art of Modeling with Spreadsheets_, 5e.** Spreadsheet engineering, management science, and modeling craft. [Wiley](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/Business+Analytics%3A+The+Art+of+Modeling+With+Spreadsheets%2C+5th+Edition-p-9781119298335)
4. **Baker, _Optimization Modeling with Spreadsheets_, 3e.** Formulation-centered LP, NLP, IP, and network modeling. [Wiley](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/Optimization+Modeling+with+Spreadsheets%2C+3rd+Edition-p-9781118937730)

Select one as the primary text and use the others for modeling craft and cases rather than teaching through a method-by-method table of contents.

## Decision analysis, operations research, and simulation

- **Clemen & Reilly, _Making Hard Decisions with DecisionTools_, 3e.** Decision trees, probability, utility, sensitivity, and value of information. [Cengage](https://www.cengage.com/c/making-hard-decisions-with-decisiontools-3e-clemen-reilly/9780538797573/)
- **Howard & Abbas, _Foundations of Decision Analysis_.** Normative decision analysis, preference, information, and decision quality; pair with Howard's [Decision Analysis: Practice and Promise](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.34.6.679).
- **Hillier & Lieberman, _Introduction to Operations Research_.** A complete OR path from defining the problem through validation and implementation. [McGraw Hill](https://www.mheducation.com/highered/product/Introduction-to-Operations-Research-Hillier.html)
- **Boyd & Vandenberghe, _Convex Optimization_.** Convexity, duality, optimality, and nonlinear-model boundaries; the authors provide a legally accessible online edition and materials. [Stanford](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/)
- **Law, _Simulation Modeling and Analysis_, 6e.** Input modeling, verification/validation, output analysis, experimental design, and simulation optimization. [McGraw Hill](https://www.mheducation.com/highered/mhp/product/simulation-modeling-analysis-sixth-edition.html)

## Uncertainty and robustness

- Shapiro, Dentcheva & Ruszczyński, _Lectures on Stochastic Programming_, 3e: scenarios, recourse, risk, SAA, and multistage decisions. [SIAM DOI](https://doi.org/10.1137/1.9781611976595)
- Ben-Tal, El Ghaoui & Nemirovski, _Robust Optimization_: tractable protection without requiring a full probability distribution. [Princeton University Press](https://press.princeton.edu/books/hardcover/9780691143682/robust-optimization)
- Bertsimas & Sim, [The Price of Robustness](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/opre.1030.0065): tune conservatism and make the nominal-performance/protection trade-off explicit.

## Data-driven prescriptions and AI frontier

- Bertsimas & Kallus, [From Predictive to Prescriptive Analytics](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.2018.3253): connect data and machine learning to prescriptions.
- Elmachtoub & Grigas, [Smart “Predict, then Optimize”](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.2020.3922): evaluate prediction through downstream decision error.
- Qi & Shen, [Integrating Prediction/Estimation and Optimization](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/educ.2022.0249): an operations-management tutorial.
- Mandi et al., [Decision-Focused Learning: Foundations, Benchmark and Opportunities](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.13565): methods, benchmarks, and limitations.

These are D4/D5 extensions, not a requirement to implement end-to-end differentiable optimization in the intensive course.

## Model reliability and governance

- Panko, [Spreadsheet Errors: What We Know. What We Think We Can Do](https://arxiv.org/abs/0802.3457): errors are common and hard to detect, supporting code-style inspection and independent recomputation.
- Panko, [What We Don't Know About Spreadsheet Errors Today](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.02601): challenges overconfidence in spreadsheet correctness.
- Saltelli et al., [Five ways to ensure that models serve society](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-020-01812-9): transparency, humility, assumption disclosure, and attention to values.

## Problem-oriented pedagogy

- Barrows, [A taxonomy of problem-based learning methods](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2923.1986.tb01386.x): PBL is not one method; problem structure and facilitation must match learning goals.
- Hmelo-Silver, [Problem-Based Learning: What and How Do Students Learn?](https://doi.org/10.1023/B:EDPR.0000034022.16470.F3): complex problems, collaboration, self-directed inquiry, reflection, and facilitation form the learning cycle.
- Powell, [Teaching Modeling in Management Science](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/ited.1.2.62): managers are decision makers and learners; sophisticated algorithms should not suppress modeling judgment.
