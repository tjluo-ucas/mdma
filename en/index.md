<section class="section">
  <div class="section-head"><p class="kicker">Problem first</p><h2>Decide what problem deserves solving before choosing a model</h2><p>A model is an inspectable contract among the decision maker, the real system, and the evidence—not a magical spreadsheet that emits the answer.</p></div>
  <div class="layer-grid">
    <article class="layer-card"><span class="number">01</span><h3>Frame</h3><p>Decision owner, objective, horizon, alternatives, boundary, and success.</p></article>
    <article class="layer-card"><span class="number">02</span><h3>Structure</h3><p>Inputs, decisions, objective, constraints, uncertainty, units, assumptions.</p></article>
    <article class="layer-card"><span class="number">03</span><h3>Solve</h3><p>DCF, LP, network, MIP, simulation, and an appropriate solver.</p></article>
    <article class="layer-card"><span class="number">04</span><h3>Challenge</h3><p>Hand checks, residuals, boundaries, sensitivity, counterexamples, infeasibility.</p></article>
    <article class="layer-card"><span class="number">05</span><h3>Act</h3><p>Recommendation, thresholds, risk, implementation conditions, owners, review.</p></article>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <div class="section-head"><p class="kicker">Core and frontier</p><h2>From spreadsheet craft to responsible prescriptive analytics</h2><p>Classical management science supplies the reliable core. Uncertainty, data-driven optimization, and model governance explain how models fail, evolve, and remain accountable in modern organizations.</p></div>
  <div class="card-grid">
    <article class="info-card"><span class="tag">Model craft</span><h3>Reliable spreadsheets</h3><p>Separate inputs, model, checks, scenarios, and outputs; trace formulas, maintain units, and independently recompute critical results.</p></article>
    <article class="info-card"><span class="tag">Optimization</span><h3>Optimization is not an oracle</h3><p>Verify domains, constraints, feasibility, optimality or gap, and translate shadow prices and trade-offs into business language.</p></article>
    <article class="info-card"><span class="tag">Uncertainty</span><h3>Risk and robustness</h3><p>Move from sensitivity and Monte Carlo to stochastic and robust choices, exposing the price of protection against change.</p></article>
    <article class="info-card"><span class="tag">AI + OR</span><h3>Prediction serves decisions</h3><p>Distinguish prediction error from decision loss. AI proposes models; people retain responsibility for values, constraints, validation, and action.</p></article>
  </div>
</section>
