# Curriculum research note

The curriculum was revised on 2026-08-21 against publisher pages, original journal articles, professional-society resources, and foundational problem-based-learning research. The public reading list links each source directly.

The revision keeps the approved six-module intensive schedule and assessment policy. It adds three non-credit extension lenses: robust/stochastic decisions, data-driven prescriptive analytics, and model governance. These extensions are not silently inserted into graded cases.

On 2026-08-21, a 31-page controlled instructor reference answer was reviewed end to end. It covers six formal cases, five group exercises, alternative solution methods, classroom discussion, and AI/ML integration. The public curriculum incorporates its pedagogical findings without reproducing protected numerical answers: tool neutrality; explicit “why this model” reasoning; method comparison; parameter/structure/objective/data/implementation variants; and the distinction between model-internal optimality and real-world decision quality.
