# Curriculum research note

The curriculum was revised on 2026-08-21 against publisher pages, original journal articles, professional-society resources, and foundational problem-based-learning research. The public reading list links each source directly.

The revision keeps the approved six-module intensive schedule and assessment policy. It adds three non-credit extension lenses: robust/stochastic decisions, data-driven prescriptive analytics, and model governance. These extensions are not silently inserted into graded cases.
